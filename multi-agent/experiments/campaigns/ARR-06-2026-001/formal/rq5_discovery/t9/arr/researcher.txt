**Research Question:**  
To what extent does distributional alignment (`EXP_DIST`) drive optimization performance (`EXP_OPT`) relative to capacity constraints (`EXP_CAP`) and coverage expansion (`EXP_COV`) within the `arr06-benchmark-v1` framework?

**Hypothesis:**  
Distributional alignment (`EXP_DIST`) yields significantly higher optimization performance (`EXP_OPT`) than capacity-constrained approaches (`EXP_CAP`) and placebo controls (`EXP_PLACEBO`), independent of coverage levels (`EXP_COV`).

**Competing Hypotheses:**  
1. **Capacity Sufficiency:** Performance gains are fully explained by capacity constraints (`EXP_CAP`); `EXP_DIST` provides no marginal improvement over `EXP_CAP` when coverage is controlled.  
2. **Coverage Confounding:** Improvements in `EXP_OPT` are driven exclusively by increased coverage (`EXP_COV`); neither `EXP_DIST` nor `EXP_CAP` exerts an independent effect once coverage is accounted for.

**Discriminative Experiment:**  
Execute a factorial comparison on `arr06-benchmark-v1` isolating `EXP_DIST` and `EXP_CAP` while stratifying or normalizing `EXP_COV` to ensure equivalence across conditions. Include `EXP_PLACEBO` as the null baseline. Primary metric: `EXP_OPT`. Secondary check: verify `EXP_COV` stability across groups.

**Predicted Outcomes:**  
If the hypothesis holds, `EXP_DIST` will demonstrate statistically significant superiority in `EXP_OPT` compared to both `EXP_CAP` and `EXP_PLACEBO`, with no significant difference between `EXP_CAP` and `EXP_PLACEBO` under matched `EXP_COV`.

**Falsification Condition:**  
The hypothesis is falsified if `EXP_DIST` fails to outperform `EXP_PLACEBO` or shows no significant difference from `EXP_CAP` under matched `EXP_COV` conditions, or if `EXP_COV` accounts for all variance in `EXP_OPT`, rendering `EXP_DIST` redundant.
