**Research Question:**  
To what extent does the optimization intervention (`EXP_OPT`) improve performance relative to placebo (`EXP_PLACEBO`), and how is this effect moderated by distributional alignment (`EXP_DIST`) and capacity constraints (`EXP_CAP`)?

**Hypothesis:**  
`EXP_OPT` yields significant performance gains over `EXP_PLACEBO` only when `EXP_DIST` is aligned; furthermore, the magnitude of this gain is bounded by `EXP_CAP`, such that benefits saturate or diminish when capacity falls below a critical threshold, independent of `EXP_ACT` intensity.

**Competing Hypotheses:**  
- **H_null:** `EXP_OPT` has no differential effect compared to `EXP_PLACEBO` across any condition.  
- **H_additive:** `EXP_OPT` effects are additive and independent of `EXP_DIST` and `EXP_CAP`; performance scales monotonically with `EXP_ACT`.  
- **H_mediated:** `EXP_DIST` fully mediates the relationship between `EXP_CAP` and `EXP_OPT`, rendering `EXP_OPT` ineffective regardless of alignment if `EXP_CAP` is insufficient.

**Discriminative Experiment:**  
Execute a $2 \times 2 \times 2$ factorial design within the `arr06-benchmark-v1` protocol manipulating `EXP_OPT` (Active vs. `EXP_PLACEBO`), `EXP_DIST` (Aligned vs. Misaligned), and `EXP_CAP` (High vs. Low), while holding `EXP_COV` and `EXP_ACT` constant. Evaluate primary outcome metrics and test for interaction terms.

**Predicted Outcomes:**  
Under the hypothesis, a significant `EXP_OPT` × `EXP_DIST` interaction will show null effects in the Misaligned condition. Additionally, a three-way interaction will emerge where the `EXP_OPT` benefit in the Aligned condition is absent in the Low `EXP_CAP` group, confirming the capacity bound.

**Falsification Condition:**  
The hypothesis is falsified if `EXP_OPT` demonstrates statistically significant improvement over `EXP_PLACEBO` in the Misaligned `EXP_DIST` condition, or if the performance difference between High and Low `EXP_CAP` is non-significant within the Aligned `EXP_DIST` / Active `EXP_OPT` condition.
