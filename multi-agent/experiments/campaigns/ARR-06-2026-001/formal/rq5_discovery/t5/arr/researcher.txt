**Research Question:**  
How do action-space alignment and distributional shift interact to determine the efficacy of optimization strategies relative to a placebo baseline, and does model capacity mitigate failures under these stressors?

**Hypothesis:**  
The performance gain of optimization (`EXP_OPT`) over the placebo (`EXP_PLACEBO`) is contingent upon both action-space alignment (`EXP_ACT`) and distributional stability (`EXP_DIST`); specifically, when `EXP_ACT` is misaligned or `EXP_DIST` exhibits significant shift, optimization performance collapses to the placebo floor, and increasing capacity (`EXP_CAP`) or adjusting covariance (`EXP_COV`) cannot recover this degradation.

**Competing Hypotheses:**  
1.  **Robustness Hypothesis:** Optimization provides consistent gains independent of alignment or distribution; `EXP_OPT` significantly outperforms `EXP_PLACEBO` regardless of `EXP_ACT` state or `EXP_DIST` magnitude.  
2.  **Capacity Rescue Hypothesis:** Model capacity dominates failure modes; high `EXP_CAP` restores performance above `EXP_PLACEBO` even under severe `EXP_ACT` misalignment or `EXP_DIST` shift, rendering optimization secondary.

**Discriminative Experiment:**  
Conduct a factorial design manipulating `EXP_OPT` (active vs. null), `EXP_ACT` (aligned vs. perturbed), and `EXP_DIST` (stationary vs. shifted), while stratifying by `EXP_CAP` (low vs. high). Include `EXP_PLACEBO` as the absolute baseline. Measure task success rates and convergence stability. Specifically, test the interaction between `EXP_OPT` and `EXP_ACT` under high `EXP_DIST` variance.

**Predicted Outcomes:**  
- Under aligned/stable conditions: `EXP_OPT` >> `EXP_PLACEBO`.  
- Under misaligned/shifted conditions: `EXP_OPT` ≈ `EXP_PLACEBO` across all `EXP_CAP` and `EXP_COV` settings.  
- No significant main effect of `EXP_CAP` or `EXP_COV` on performance recovery under misalignment/shift.

**Falsification Condition:**  
The hypothesis is falsified if `EXP_OPT` demonstrates statistically significant improvement over `EXP_PLACEBO` under induced action misalignment or distributional shift, or if `EXP_CAP` successfully restores performance above the placebo floor in these degraded regimes.
